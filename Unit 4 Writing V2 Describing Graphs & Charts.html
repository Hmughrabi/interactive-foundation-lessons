Describing Graphs & Charts

Learn how to identify key information, compare figures and write a clear description of data.

1. Understanding the Data

The charts describe rainfall and temperature in Tehran during one year. Study the figures before reading the model answer.

Bar chart · Rainfall in Tehran (mm)

Selected figures from the lesson: December has the highest rainfall (40 mm). June and July have the lowest rainfall (2 mm).

Line graph · Temperature in Tehran (°C)

Selected figures from the lesson: July is the hottest month (40°C). January, February and December share the minimum (10°C).

Important: These charts display the exact numerical figures stated in the original lesson. The other comparisons in the lesson are presented in words because the original page does not provide a complete set of monthly numerical values.

A simple writing plan

1. What?Identify what the graphs show: rainfall and temperatures.
2. Where?Identify the place: Tehran.
3. When?Identify the time: over one year.

Model Answer

The graphs show rainfall and temperatures in Tehran over one year.

First, the bar chart shows the average rainfall in millimetres. The highest rainfall is in December. It reaches 40 mm. December is the rainiest month. The lowest rainfall is in June and July. It falls to 2 mm. June and July are the driest months. July is drier than August. November is rainier than September.

Next, the line graph shows the average temperature in degrees Celsius. The maximum temperature is in July. It rises to 40°C, making July the hottest month. The minimum temperature is 10°C in January, February and December. These are the coolest months. August is hotter than October. The temperature in March is as warm as that in November.

Overall, July is the hottest and one of the driest months, while December is the rainiest month. The figures show clear differences between the seasons.

Remember: Describe what the figures show. Do not add opinions or information that cannot be seen in the graphs.

2. Key Vocabulary

Learn these words and expressions. They help you describe numbers and compare information.

Rainfall
The amount of rain that falls.
Temperature
How hot or cold something is.
Highest / Maximum
The greatest value.
Lowest / Minimum
The smallest value.
Wettest / Rainiest
With the most rain.
Driest
With the least rain.
Hottest
With the highest temperature.
Coolest
With the lowest temperature.
Reaches
Gets to a particular number.
Falls to / Drops to
Goes down to a particular number.
Rainier than
Having more rain than another place or time.
As warm as
Having the same temperature as something else.
